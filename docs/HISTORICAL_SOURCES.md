# Historical sources

## Ancient evidence

Ludus Latrunculorum is an ancient game known from fragmentary evidence. Seneca is part of the ancient literary record. A complete digital rules specification cannot be inferred from his name or the surviving references alone.

## Scholarly reconstruction

The project's authority is the Seneca section of the [Locus Ludi English rules publication](https://locusludi.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/LUDUS-LATRUNCULORUM_rules_GB.pdf), including its diagrams. The Piso reconstruction is a separate ruleset. Verification of the Seneca section is pending access to the publication.

[Locus Ludi's game index](https://locusludi.ch/play-ancient-online-games/) and [Ludii's variant listing](https://ludii.games/variantDetails.php?keyword=Ludus+Latrunculorum&variant=1588) are secondary cross-checks. The primary publication takes precedence if implementations disagree.

## Digital conventions

The project brief proposes explicit handling of chained leaps, custody and rescue, and repetition. These proposals are implementation decisions pending comparison with the primary publication. Threefold repetition is a modern draw safeguard.
